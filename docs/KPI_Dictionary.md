# RevOps360 KPI & Metric Definitions

### 1. Win Rate (%)
- **Definition:** Percentage of closed deals successfully converted to revenue.
- **Formula:** `(Count of Closed Won Deals / Total Closed Deals [Won + Lost]) * 100`

### 2. Weighted Pipeline Forecast ($)
- **Definition:** Probability-adjusted expected revenue based on deal stage progress.
- **Formula:** `Sum(Deal Amount * Stage Probability)`

### 3. Sales Cycle Time (Days)
- **Definition:** Average number of days taken to move an opportunity from creation to won.
- **Formula:** `Average(Close Date - Created Date)` for Closed Won deals.
